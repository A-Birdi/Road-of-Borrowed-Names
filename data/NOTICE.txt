stroke-order demonstrations)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Source:     KanjiVG, https://kanjivg.tagaini.net
            https://github.com/KanjiVG/kanjivg

Copyright:  Copyright (C) 2009/2010/2011 Ulrich Apel and the KanjiVG project.
Licence:    Creative Commons Attribution-Share Alike 3.0 Unported (CC BY-SA 3.0),
            https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/
Covers:     the 164 hiragana and katakana (basic, voiced, semi-voiced, small
            forms, the letter vu and the long-vowel mark) and 1,548 kanji:
            every kanji the game's text uses, and the first 33 the pad read,
            for the handwriting pad, its numbered stroke-order demonstrations
            and the kanji chart.
Changes:    The embedded stroke data (RB.recogData; src/recog/10_strokedata.js in
            the source tree) is a derived, adapted form of the KanjiVG SVG
            files: each stroke path was sampled into a point sequence,
            simplified, quantised to integers in KanjiVG's 109x109 box and
            packed into a compact text encoding (kanji points as steps from
            the one before). Stroke order and stroke direction are unchanged.
            Each kanji's radical, as KanjiVG marks it, is kept alongside (used
            only to sort kanji into themes in the chart). This adapted data is
            distributed under the same licence, CC BY-SA 3.0.
