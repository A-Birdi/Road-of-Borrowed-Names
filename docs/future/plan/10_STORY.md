# 10 · Story: more chapters, the Hush, companions and the postgame

*Expansion plan, draft 1 (2026-10-07). Planning only. Spoiler-safe: the existing Chapters 3–6 appear only as the
spec describes them. Every decision that depends on their contents points to a numbered note in
[SEALED_STORY_NOTES.md](SEALED_STORY_NOTES.md), which you should not open until you've finished the game.*

## 1. Robin's direction

- **Length is not a hard cap** [R0]. "We can bring it up to a solid 10, or double it to 12 if we want The Hush to
  feel more epic, something drowned in folklore and mystery, and for us to ultimately remove the threat of the Hush
  from the world, while still maintaining our problem solving adventure thereafter" [R1].
- **More main chapters are "not critical, but a desire for later"** [R2 §10].
- **Robin's working assumption** [R2 §10]: dispatching the Hush by the end can still leave lingering effects in
  regions not yet resolved. "If the story differs from my assumption here, it can be reviewed later." Sealed note
  S10 compares this with canon. In short, the assumption **fits the existing ending well enough to build on**,
  with one nuance recorded there.
- **Other side regions are welcome as true postgame content** [R1].

## 3. How to add chapters: four options

| Option | Shape | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Insert before the finale** | New chapters between existing ones; the Archive stays last | Makes the journey, and the Hush, larger, as Robin wants; keeps the ending | Renumbering; saves already past the inserted points (§9); later chapters' references (sealed S1) |
| **B. Add after the ending** | An "Act II" | Simple for saves | Breaks "remains resolved"; Robin and Astra both warn against undoing the victory. **Not recommended** |
| **C. Postgame only** | Keep six chapters; all new regions are postgame | No spec change; simplest | The main story doesn't grow; the Hush is no more "epic" |
| **D. Hybrid** (**recommended**) | A + C: four new main chapters before the finale; the island as a side region; the Hall and the new settlement postgame | Robin's whole picture | The most work |

| **3** | **Manybridge: the Exchange** | **new** | Bridges forget their names; a canal city's trade breaks down; the first sign the phenomenon spreads through *printed* names |

| 7 | Lanternfall | existing (was 5) | The spec's: an administrative town where disagreement is disappearing from speech |
| **8** | **The Cloudroad** | **new** | A mountain highway of post stations whose lanterns fail; messages garbled from station to station; the last courier; the Mist Barrier |

| 10 | The Still Archive | existing (was 6) | The spec's: the final dungeon and climax |

**Why this order, without spoilers.**
- Two new chapters early bring a city's scale and the first hints that the phenomenon is old and widespread (the
  printers' blocks, folklore).
- Two new chapters late lengthen the approach to the finale, which is Robin's "more epic". They also give the
  companion a social, lively stretch before the end, distinct from the spec's quiet stretch in Snowbell.
- **Sealed note S1** checks the order against what the existing chapters reveal. **Sealed note S3** covers the
  Cloudroad's geography.

**Side region:** Kotonoha, early by the player's boat (around new Chapters 6–7), and for everyone by public ferry
from new Chapter 8.

## 5. The twelve-chapter variant

If Robin wants twelve:
- **11. The Keepers' Road** (new). The old lantern-keepers' order: where Tsuru's teacher learned; how the keepers
  first named しじま; why the Hundred Tales hall was abandoned. A chapter of folklore and archives that makes the
  Hush's mystery deeper and older, without changing what it is. Placement and canon fit: sealed note S2.
- **12. Kotonoha as a main chapter.** The island's normal-access visit becomes a full chapter, instead of a side
  region with a ferry.

**Trade-off.** Twelve chapters make the main story long. The spec's 10–15 hour target (line 36) would need
revising
upwards, and with it Robin's patience for pacing in a full playthrough. **Recommendation: build ten; decide on
twelve after playing ten.**

## 6. Making the Hush "more epic" without changing what it is

- **Seed folklore early and widely.** Every new region adds a telling of the keepers' old warnings (C9). The
  mystery grows by *questions*, not by new answers that compete with the existing chapters' reveals (sealed S1).
- **Scale up.** Village (1), harbour (2), **a whole city's printed names** (3–4), and a highway that links regions
  (8). The threat feels larger because its reach is larger.
- **Personal stakes in each new chapter** (a courier's message, a theatre's play, an inn's guest), so scale never
  becomes abstract.
- **Never re-explain.** New chapters show effects; the existing chapters keep their revelations.

## 7. Companions in the new chapters

- **Personal quests stay as they are.** Each new chapter adds **one companion beat per companion**: a scene, a
  choice and an illustration page. Details per region are in [07_REGIONS.md](07_REGIONS.md).
- **The Bond table** (exact, capped at 12) gets new events from the new chapters. Rebalance it so the cap still
  arrives late and naturally, without inflating (C-32).
- **Canon notes kept:**
  - Nao and Ren use they/them (Nao uses 俺 in Japanese); Mio and Suzu use she/her.
  - Suzu wants a double-act partner rather than stardom (C-31).
  - No romance, jealousy or resentment wording (an existing test enforces this).
  - Unchosen candidates remain people in the world and never resent the player.

## 8. A temporary separation (Robin's allowance)

**Robin's rule** [R2 §1]. The troupe can't die or be permanently separated. A companion may stay behind, be
captured, or be briefly incapacitated, then reunite soon after. The solo stretch must be playable without them
(Astra).

**Proposal: surety at the Mist Barrier (the Cloudroad).**
- The checkpoint's keeper will let the party through only once their blanked travel papers are verified. Until
  then, **one traveller must stay as surety**: an old, non-violent custom with a real flavour. The companion
  volunteers.
- **The player crosses alone.** They fetch proof from the next station and come back. It is a short solo section of
  about two maps:
  - the companion's battle support is replaced by **the environment** (a stationed shelter);
  - the **checkpoint's runner** acts as a guest (E1), with their own agenda;
  - the **player's notes** stand in for the companion's hints.
- **The reunion** is a written scene per companion, with a travel-volume page.
- **No techniques** while apart, since a technique needs two (E18). The solo encounters are tuned for one.
- **The spec amendment needed:** "exactly two adventurers, except a short authored separation with reunion" (C-12).

## 9. Existing saves and the inserted chapters

**Robin's own save will be finished** before any of this ships, and so will many players'. Their options, for
Robin to choose (C-02):

| Option | A save already past an inserted chapter… | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|
| **i. Detour** | …can travel to the new region and play its story as a "story from the road", with dialogue framed for its phase (S1) | No restart; uses the adaptive-region machinery | Each inserted chapter needs an "after the fact" framing: more writing; some beats feel out of order |
| **ii. Chapter Journey** (K6) | …can start a new campaign in a free slot at the new chapter's start, carrying learning, appearance, keepsakes and records; earlier choices take documented defaults ("a retelling") | Plays the chapters as written | Uses a slot; defaults instead of the player's own choices |
| **iii. New Game+** (exists) | …plays the expanded story from the start in NG+ | Simplest; already exists | Replays the whole game |
| **iv. Postgame visit only** | …can visit the new regions in their *after* state and do their side content; the main chapters only in a new or NG+ campaign | Little extra writing | The story chapters are not playable from that save |

**Recommendation:** **iv + ii.** Every save can visit the new regions and do side content at once. The story
chapters are playable through a Chapter Journey or NG+. That way no save is ever forced to restart, and nothing is
invented about the past.

## 10. The postgame, expanded

After the ending ("The story is over. The roads go on."), in addition to what exists:
- **The Hall of a Hundred Tales** (D9);
- **Atlas commissions**: practice, themed and survey (D7);
- **Superbosses** (E16);
- **The new settlement** (07_REGIONS.md R7);
- **Kotonoha's matured state** and deepest grove;
- the **festival hall**, the **boat** and its ports;
- the final editions of Manybridge's broadsheet; the checkpoint teahouse.

None of it reopens the threat. Lingering effects, discoveries, disputes and new places carry the adventure, which
is Robin's and Astra's shared position.

## 11. Length (no claims)

- The spec's target is 10–15 hours for a first playthrough (line 36), stated as a target, not a measurement.
- Four new chapters, each roughly an existing chapter's size, would add a comparable amount. That is a target to
  verify with timed play, never claimed from estimates (spec: "never claim measured playtime without evidence").
- L19's instrumentation counts content per chapter so the pacing can be compared fairly.
