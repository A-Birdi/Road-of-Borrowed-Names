# U00 · The Wayfarer's Ledger as it stands

*Expansion playbook §15A, packet U00 (authorised by Robin's C-78, 2026-10-09). The inventory taken before the book
interface, so nothing is lost on the way. Generated by `node tests/e2e/ui_inventory.mjs`; the full record, control by
control, is [ui_inventory.json](ui_inventory.json). Captures: `docs/screenshots/book/baseline/`.*

## How it was taken

- **Fixture shaped like Robin's save** (the two screenshots of 2026-10-09): Saltglass, the main road "Labels That
  Disagree" at its ninth step ("go deeper into the archive") and followed, two side requests ("The Way to the Cove",
  "A Nameplate of Sea Glass"), Reedwake's three main quests completed, Suzu, a cat called Samson, 3:27 played.
- **Every section and every sub-page** of the Ledger was opened at **1440×900** and at **375×667** (a phone, touch).
  On each, every control was recorded: its words, its action (`data-*`), its state (pressed, selected, checked,
  disabled), its size, and which part of the book it sits in.
- **Framed boxes** were counted on each page: elements inside the pages with their own border, shadow or a
  background unlike their parent's. It is a rough measure of the "rectangles inside rectangles" Robin described,
  kept so the book can be compared with today.

## Fonts the browser really drew with

Read with the browser's own font report (`CSS.getPlatformFontsForNode`) in the test container. **The game embeds no
font**: each role names a list of system fonts, and the device picks. These are the container's fonts; on Robin's
Windows PC the same lists resolve to Palatino Linotype, Segoe UI and Yu Gothic or Meiryo, and on Android to the
system serif, Roboto and Noto Sans CJK. So today the game looks different on Robin's two devices.

## Found during the inventory

- **The two "Next" lines in Robin's Journey screenshot are one destination said twice.** The step "go deeper into the
  archive" has two guidance targets, the Tide Clerk and the doorway strip in front of them, and both lead to the same
  scene (`sg.da_boss` in `sg.da_vault`). The guidance writes a line per target. Fix (with a test): merge targets that
  lead to the same scene on the same map into one line. Whether "no way there from here just now" was right for
  Robin depends on where Robin stood; from the harbour in this fixture it is, because the way back in is not open.
- **The Ledger has five sections today.** Distractions (K7) is planned, not built; the book shows the five that exist
  and makes room for a sixth.
- **Not in this inventory:** the title screen and Inn Ledger, Settings, dialogue, battle and the writing pad. Their
  packets (U04, U05) come later; the same tool will inventory them first.
