# Cosmetic pets — captures

Real captures of the built game in headless Chromium (synthetic test campaigns in fresh browser
contexts; no player saves), made by `tests/e2e/pets.mjs` and `tests/e2e/pets_video.mjs`, copied here
as WebP at full scale by `tests/e2e/pets_docs.mjs` (pixel art is never smoothed). Some are cropped to
the part of the screen that matters.

| File | What it shows |
|---|---|
| `cat_world_follow.webp` | Reedwake square after a walk with the keyboard: the cat has followed Mio along the walked trail and settled on a free tile beside the two of you. |
| `cat_world_bridge.webp` | Crossing the Reedwake bridge: the cat follows on the planks behind Mio, never on the water. |
| `cat_battle_calm.webp` | The Flour Moth on the mill road, calm while you choose: the cat sits between Mio and you, clear of the cards, the slips and the resolve panel. |
| `cat_battle_react.webp` | Mid-exchange (Unravel): the word on paper at the moth, the cat's attention on the thread (its unravel reaction). |
| `cat_company_wide.webp` | Company › Pet at 1280×800: the met animals as cards (Select / Selected, No pet), the detail leaf with portrait, live preview, where you met, Rename, Appearance and the plain "Cosmetic companion — no battle or puzzle effects" note. |
| `cat_company_pat.webp` | The Pat preview: your hand (in your skin tone) and the cat leaning into it, eyes closed. Nothing is gained. |
| `cat_company_390.webp`, `cat_company_320_200.webp` | The phone page (390×844) and 320×640 at 200 % text: the detail as one page with Back, no sideways scrolling. |
| `cat_vignette_cause.webp` | "A Dry Corner", Reedwake: the ginger cat creeping toward the carpenter's dry corner, the loose reed screen that keeps knocking it away. |

| `cat_vignette_name.webp` | Naming at acquisition: plain text, any language, up to 24 characters, the default (Koma) kept with one button. |
| `cat_vignette_after.webp` | After "Travel together": the cat that was in the corner is now the one following you. |
