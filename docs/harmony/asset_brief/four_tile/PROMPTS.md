# Four-tile Harmony sheets: Nao, Mio and Ren

Robin's four-tile Suzu sheet (2026-10-04) is the approved style and framing. The game can turn a sheet like it into
the full seven-frame cut-in: Suzu's code-drawn sequence was measured from that sheet. These prompts ask an image
tool for the same kind of sheet for the other three companions.

**Attach to each request, in this order:**
1. **Robin's Suzu four-tile sheet**: the style lock. Robin's copy; **never committed**.
2. **`ref_four_tile_<name>.png`** (this folder): identity, the four poses in Suzu's framing, the rules.
3. *Optional:* `../ref_companion_<name>.png`, the full game sheet for that character.

**What to send back:** one 2 × 2 sheet per character, the same size and layout as the Suzu sheet (four equal tiles,
each 6 : 5, about 687 × 572 px). Real transparency, or flat `#FF00FF` magenta. No text. A ZIP is not needed.

The reference pages are the pose guides; the diagrams on them are deliberately plain and are **not** the style.

---

## Nao (they; courier) — Read the Opening

Paste this with the Suzu sheet and `ref_four_tile_nao.png`:

> Create a 2 × 2 sprite sheet of four animation keyframes for an original pixel-art character, Nao, in exactly
> the style, finish and framing of the attached Suzu sheet: crisp square pixels, rich painted anime shading,
> a selective dark outline that lightens inside shapes, warm light from the upper left, and a cool lavender rim
> light on the outer edges. Same scale and placement as Suzu in every tile: a bust, head right of centre, the
> shoulders running off the bottom edge, with the hair and gesture flowing to the left. Transparent background.
> No text, grid, borders or labels.
>
> Nao is a courier: medium brown skin, spiky dark-brown hair, a yellow pencil with a pink eraser tucked behind the
> left ear, a mustard-yellow scarf with a tail, an olive-green tunic, and the leather strap of a big satchel
> across the chest. Match the second attached page for identity and colour. Nao is the same person in all four
> tiles; use they/them.
>
> The four tiles are one continuous, practical, assured motion. Purposeful, never showy:
> 1. Top left, "reading the road": focused and still, eyes cut to the left, brows level and low, mouth set;
>    the near hand's thumb hooked under the satchel strap.
> 2. Top right, "found it": the glance sharpens, eyes open wider, lips part; the hand leaves the strap and two
>    fingers begin to point up and to the left; the scarf tail starts to swing.
> 3. Bottom left, "Read the Opening", the hero pose: a precise point with index and middle finger held
>    together, arm extended to the far left edge; head turned to the viewer with an assured lopsided grin and
>    one brow slightly raised; a single small gold glint at the fingertips.
> 4. Bottom right, "noted": the pointing hand back on the strap, the other hand tapping the pencil behind the
>    ear; an easy half-smile with relaxed eyelids; the scarf settles.
>
> Keep the head within a few pixels of the same place in all four tiles. Do not add weapons, jewellery or new
> props, and do not copy Suzu's open-palm fling or wink.

## When the sheets come back

- Send each sheet back as it is. The in-between frames are drawn in code from the four tiles, so the tool does
  not need to make them.
- Each sheet is then redrawn in code at 192 × 160, frame by frame, the way Suzu's was, for Robin to judge.
- The reference pages are regenerated with `node tests/e2e/harmony_four_tile_refs.mjs` (poses in
  `tools/harmony/four_tile_specs.mjs`; identity and colours come from `../ref_companion_<name>.png`).
