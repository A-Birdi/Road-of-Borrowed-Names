Ensemble recordings

Not yet listened to by anyone. MT-2 (C-92) recorded every song the game plays in the Ensemble treatment. The two pilots (Snowbell, the Keepers' Road battle) were heard by Robin on 2026-10-12 (their captures). The others were made the same way, with each part's balance set by measurement against the Original and voices chosen by the lead for the instruments the pilots did not use; nobody has heard them yet. What each is made of is listed under it.

Each song's players are the recording's own files, as the game fetches them (Ogg Opus): the first pass from the cue's start to its join, then the steady cycle that repeats (a cue that does not loop is one file). They play at the level they were mastered at; in the game a measured trim matches the Original's loudness (shown). The comparisons below were rendered by the game's own code through its own audio graph. Measurements: docs/music/ensemble/qa/<song>.json.
